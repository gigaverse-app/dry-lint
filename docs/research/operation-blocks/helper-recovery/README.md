# Complete helpers recover useful operations across region boundaries

An additional complete-helper representation recovers useful shared operations that the original operation-block comparer misses. In the selected balanced Haiku panel, newly admitted pairs produced **10 bounded-useful reports**, while displaced prior pairs included **one bounded-useful report**: a net gain of **nine**. The gain has a precision and runtime cost. Five new reports were unsupported by independent source review, and indexing and neighbour queries took longer. This supports keeping the improved comparer opt-in, with defaults unchanged.

This addendum extends the [original operation-block study](../README.md); it does not revise that frozen comparison. The [first-round recordings and provider-free replay](r1/README.md) preserve the initial Sonnet/Jev diagnostic. The [production parity and runtime appendix](production-parity-runtime.md) describes the implementation checks and their limits. The [balanced evidence and replay](balanced/README.md) supply exact requests, source decisions and native admission states. The [public-source extension](public-extension/README.md) records the additional source contexts separately from the original sweep freeze.

## Why a complete helper helps

The original comparer splits functions at control-flow boundaries and keeps the first 12 eligible complete regions. A small helper can express a useful operation across those boundaries even though none of its individual regions meets the size and concrete-call guards. Across the native-source corpus available at the original sweep freeze, 13,205 eligible whole helpers had no original region. Only 93 functions exceeded the first-12 cap. Repeated region postings suppressed 75 anchors that would fit a unique-parent cap, affecting 2,569 parent documents. These observations motivated testing helper coverage; they do not establish that the original caps or posting semantics were bugs.

The treatment first runs the original comparer and returns its eligible unseen winner unchanged. Only when that route abstains does a separate index search the original regions plus at most one complete helper body. The extra route has at most 13 regions per function, eight anchors, 64 region postings per anchor and 64 candidate parents. Original floors and guards remain unchanged. The executable helper body supplies retrieval tokens, while the saved Candidate and Claude questions retain the full native function, including its name, signature and documentation. There is no residual-coverage veto and no Jev gate on Claude judging.

For example, `realtime_ssl_for_url` and `async_responses_websocket` have whole-function cosine .237118, below the .35 exploration floor. The helper has no original eligible region, but its complete body produces a .748934 region match. Independent source review accepts a shared operation that turns an already-resolved False SSL value into a fresh unverified TLS context. It does **not** accept replacing the websocket caller's entire SSL branch with the existing URL helper: that would change the `ws://` early return, the `wss://` guard and shared-getter timing.

Streaming hook/ModelResponse accumulation and custom-key hook invocation provide two further examples. Their extraction must preserve None versus empty-string inputs, non-ModelResponse branches, fast paths, cleanup, request identity and type, hook-specific error labels, and the timing of result reads. The full scoped recommendations and original judge advice remain in the R1 source and answer recordings.

## Three comparisons, with separate meanings

The fixed original comparison is baseline top 3 + explicit TF-IDF top 20 at .35 + the original operation-block plugin, under the original estimated $0.04 concept-policy ledger. The two public source pins contain 39,952 background documents, including 39,806 exact native functions. The fallback produced 164 additional directional winners, 48 funded directions and 40 distinct funded pairs. Endpoint/family sampling froze 37 new candidate pairs plus 16 controls before new judge answers. Known supported regression exposure stayed at 37/39 duplicate pair/background units and 38/80 separate units, with no gains or losses. Exposure to a known separate pair is judging work, not a false report.

The first paid diagnostic independently asked both Sonnet/shared-operations/1024 and Jev/context about all 53 pairs. At the .80 report threshold, Sonnet reported three candidate pairs and Jev three, with two overlapping. All four distinct report-bearing opportunities were source-confirmed bounded useful. Controls produced no reports. Physical spend was $0.44025052 for 106 fresh requests. This is diagnostic evidence attached to the original admission ledger, not a purchase of answers under the original concept prompt or the balanced Haiku policy.

The subsequent balanced shadow uses explicit Haiku 5.5, shared-operations, output limit 2048, report/drop thresholds .50/.50, drift threshold .80, no escalation, and native $0.04 CampaignStore admissions. Across the 53 selected query campaigns, held admissions changed from 167 to 177: 28 prior admissions were displaced and 38 were gained. The prespecified 71-case judging panel includes all 37 selected candidate gains, six admitted original controls, and every one of the 28 displaced pairs. Ten original controls were native rejections. The one additional native gain was subsequently answered and source-reviewed: Haiku scored it .20 with label `separate`, and source review classified it as related. The final table therefore covers all 38 new pairs and all 28 displaced pairs; the initial 71-case selection remains identifiable in the balanced evidence.

|---|---:|---:|
| Source-reviewed pairs | 28 | 38 |

The matched difference is **+9 bounded-useful reports**, +1 tiny-useful report, and +3 unsupported reports on these changed admissions. This table is not a precision estimate for all original or treatment reports: it covers all gains and displacements within these selected campaigns, excluding retained pairs. The extra gain adds no report, so the 17 new reports and net bounded-useful gain are unchanged. The initial 71-case execution cost $0.05256726, before the separately recorded extra-gain request: 71 fresh Haiku requests, 28 fresh Jev requests and 43 exact Jev reuses. Research spend, estimated normal charges and native held reservations are separate quantities.

The lost bounded-useful report matters. Two authentication paths repeat expiry normalization, UTC handling, strict comparison and an expired-key exception. Review supports extracting that policy while preserving malformed-date errors, redaction, debug logging and each caller's position. The fallback's high region scores can reorder exploration and displace this pair even though original plugin winners are protected. The result is a measured net gain with a known useful loss, not zero balanced report loss.

## Coverage and decision

At the original full-source sweep freeze, the replay covered all 119,312 native function documents in ten compatible matched cohorts, among 119,470 background documents. At that freeze, the other 69 matched backgrounds lacked compatible exact native Python source in the archive; this is a historical coverage limit, not the final extent of available public source. The separate [public-source extension](public-extension/README.md) now adds 14 contexts with 21,950 native function occurrences from nine pins, covering 115 row/background units or 87 units after lineage deduplication. Both fallback methods leave the extension's measured exposure unchanged. These extension counts remain separate from the original sweep and are not independent-family counts. Missing or unsupported rows were never treated as negatives. The historical inventory contains 617 records and 580 accepted function records, of which 103 accepted source records were evaluable, yielding the 119 accepted pair/background units above. Repeated revisions and pair/background units are not independent families. Firecrawl and curator were unseen; private adopter and Kaizen source remain held.

The frozen candidate split has three development pairs and 34 pairs overlapping prior source-owner/call-family proxies, with no fresh confirmation candidate family. The ten bounded-useful additions span nine proxy components: nine pair instances are regression and one is development. These new useful pair instances are valid development gains, but there is no fresh holdout win or evidence of independent-family generalization. No successor recipe or threshold search was introduced after seeing labels.

Keep the complete-helper improvement opt-in. It adds source-supported useful reports under an actual balanced judge configuration, and the implementation preserves the original comparer route. The added unsupported reports, useful displacement and measured runtime overhead argue against default enablement. The balanced run is a cold, held-reservation simulation: reservations were not settled between answers, and there is no historical PR spending or revision sequence. It does not establish deployed traffic performance.

## Performance follow-up

[Sharing operation-block preparation](performance/README.md) reduces cold construction 16–21% and standalone query time 22–38% with exact output parity on 24,135 queries. It changes runtime, not this study's recall or precision results. The follow-up includes the implementation argument, actual benchmark/parity scripts, repetitions and input-availability limits.
