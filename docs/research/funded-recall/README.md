# Funded recall research decisions

These experiments separate candidate retrieval, admission under an estimated $0.04 campaign, provider answers, and independently reviewed useful reports. They use observed development/regression evidence; they are not pristine population holdouts. A provider score is not a source label.

The research protocol requires at least $0.20 of fresh usage-priced judging per direction. A direction with no useful benefit receives one error-informed revised mechanism and another $0.20. Exact cached answers do not meet either spending minimum; every estimated campaign still charges its normal logical cost. Missing answers remain unknown. All selected controls and begun paired units are completed.

## Completed decision

[Deterministic judge context](context/PR-SUMMARY.md): stop the tested source-anchor and body-window recipes. Both rounds meet the spending floor ($0.24912180 and $0.45707392; 80 fresh calls). Neither adds a Jev report. The revised windows miss both independently confirmed novel partial operations and lose one Claude positive control report. Related and already-shared source pairs remain separate from binary negatives.

The folder contains matched outcomes, costs, source pins, exact request/output hashes, and checksums. It excludes source code, prompts, credentials, and private adopter identities. Full private replay artifacts remain archived in the lab. Hash receipts establish artifact identity but do not make omitted artifacts independently reproducible from this repository alone.

Other funded directions remain in progress and are not rejected by this decision. This evidence changes no production configuration, judge policy, or CI behavior.

Run `python3 docs/research/funded-recall/context/audit.py` from the repository root to verify the published hashes and recompute costs and paired report changes without provider calls. This checks the published evidence; it does not substitute for inspecting the archived source and prompts.
