# Stop the tested context recipes

Two funded development experiments found **no Jev report recoveries** from either deterministic source anchors or fixed body windows. Both required spend floors were met: **80 new paid calls, $0.70619572 actual** ($0.24912180 + $0.45707392). Historical spend and cached baselines are excluded. No production change or further variant is proposed.

Round 1 tested six source families with paired baseline/context calls for both models. At the frozen **0.8 reporting threshold**, both models had **0 recoveries, 0 lost reports, 0 new false reports**. All four accepted partial-operation positives remained unreported.

Round 2 completed 56 fresh calls: twelve novel candidate pairs plus revised requests for four previously accepted controls. Root verified all twelve novel source pairs and accepted **2 narrow useful operations, 6 already-shared cases, 3 related cases, and 1 negative**. The four controls add two positives and two separate cases. The nine nonbinary cases remain nonbinary. Novel labels are accepted bounded assessments, not promotions to whole-function gold; no globally new family count is claimed.
