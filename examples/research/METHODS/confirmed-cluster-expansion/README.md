# Confirmed-seed graph expansion exposes known siblings with substantial unknown burden

The extension covers 56 eligible development cohorts, 551,184 function occurrences and 7,762,130 directed edges. For 102 one-available-source seeds, reciprocal anchored expansion adds 20 accepted-registry useful exposures and reaches ten additional accepted useful components, with 2,484 unknown leads in the expanded frontier and 637,469 additional source-token proxies. Bounded diffusion reaches the same accepted increment with 2,996 unknowns and 776,094 extra proxies.

An agent already inspecting a useful pair may reuse that context to discover related operations. The comparison measures candidate exposure and source-reading burden, not completed refactorings.

## What was tested

Construct unchanged source-exact sparse/dense/block graphs. Compare pinned Detector.relatives, unseeded changed-endpoint retrieval, reciprocal anchored expansion and bounded seed diffusion. Preserve oracle seeds, intentional false seeds and exact recorded provider-positive seeds as separate authorities. Full functions remain unchanged; leads remain unreported, nonblocking and without confidence claims.

A provider-positive seed in the exploration lane can produce no native relative proposals while graph expansion exposes an already-labeled cancellation operation. This is retrospective recovery of known evidence, not a newly accepted report.
