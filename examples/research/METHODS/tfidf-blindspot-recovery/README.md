## What was tested

Normalize complete Python AST regions while preserving constants/operators/variable relationships: at most twelve regions per function, 25–400 nodes, at least two calls, 4–80 lines, postings ≤64 parents and ≤64 targets per query. Combine this with native block evidence. The frozen structural recipe requires sparse <.35 and available dense similarity ≥.65, ranked .6 structural + .4 dense. Compare raw .35/top20, deep .35/top100 and zero-floor/top100, retaining default baseline candidates. Residual/hybrid variants protect the complete raw prefix.

Matching bounded credential-acquisition/lifecycle regions can nominate a pair even when the parent functions serve different responsibilities. It still needs a compatible-contract and ownership review; shared syntax is insufficient. The normalized route produced no zero-posting cases in this study.
